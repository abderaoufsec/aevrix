# Code of conduct

Aevrix follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The short version:

- Be respectful. Disagree about code, not about people; assume good faith.
- Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and unwelcome attention
  are not tolerated, in issues, pull requests, chat or anywhere else.
- Take responsibility, apologise when you get it wrong, and give people room to
  correct themselves.
- Unwelcome behaviour gets a warning first, then consequences: no interaction,
  then a ban from project spaces.

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through GitHub rather than in a
public issue, and include what happened, when, and where. Reports are handled
confidentially and reviewed by the maintainer.
